Research

Research Focus

My research focuses on catalysts, electrolyte membranes, and interfacial engineering for high-performance and durable fuel cell and water electrolysis systems. I investigate degradation mechanisms in electrochemical energy devices and develop materials-based strategies to improve their performance and durability. In particular, I use initiated chemical vapor deposition (iCVD) to study vapor-phase polymer deposition behavior and engineer functional surfaces and interfaces for electrochemical energy applications.

Electrochemical Analysis

Electrochemical evaluation of PEMFCs, AEMWEs, and related energy-conversion devices.

Electrolyte Membranes

Polymer electrolyte membranes and reinforced composite membrane architectures.

Catalysts & Electrodes

Catalyst layers, porous electrodes, and membrane–electrode interfacial design.

iCVD Surface Engineering

iCVD-based surface functionalization, vapor-phase polymer coatings, and interfacial engineering.
